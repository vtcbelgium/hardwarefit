Frequently Asked Questions

What does the HardwareFit calculator measure?

HardwareFit estimates whether a local LLM fits your GPU or system memory. You enter VRAM, system RAM, hardware platform, and context length, and it returns per-model fit estimates for local inference.

Does HardwareFit run entirely in my browser?

Yes. The VRAM and hardware compatibility calculations run 100% client-side in your browser using local JavaScript. No computation is sent to a server.

Does HardwareFit store cookies or upload my inputs?

No. HardwareFit sets zero tracking cookies and makes zero cloud uploads. Your VRAM, RAM, and context inputs stay in volatile browser memory and are never transmitted.
